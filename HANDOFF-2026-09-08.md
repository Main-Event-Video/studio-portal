## THINGS THAT COST A RENDER TODAY, SO THEY DO NOT AGAIN

Each of these is now asserted in `verify.mjs`:

- **Valid but invisible.** Neon arcs were drawn on the edge of a box LARGER than
  the frame. At 104% the outline sits at -2% and 102% — not one pixel inside the
  picture. Arithmetic, not Creatomate. Guard: rings must be 100% of their shot.
- **Visible but invalid.** `stroke_start + length` ran past 100, which
  Creatomate rejects for the WHOLE render. Five arcs in thirty, so it was
  intermittent by construction. Guard: every stroke value in 0-100.
- **Valid-looking but wrong units.** `z_rotation: '90deg'` instead of `90°`.
  Rejects the whole render. Guard: 2955 values checked, all must end in `°`.

And two diagnostics that replaced guesswork:

- The render route now **returns the real error message** instead of a bare 500.
  It had no top-level catch, so "KEY is not defined" was invisible for hours.
- The generate line **always reports both ends**: `Sent neon:on dust:off —
  built 9 neon / 0 dust / 0 leak elements`. Its PRESENCE proves the browser has
  the current bundle; the numbers separate "never built" from "built and
  dropped". A stale bundle cost two renders before this existed.

## THE ONE THAT WILL BITE AGAIN

`Number(null) === 0` **and 0 is finite.** An `isFinite`-only guard turns "not
set" into "zero". It has now caused four separate bugs in `lib/montage.js`
(backdrop crossfade, frame thickness, atmosphere intensity twice). Always check
`null`/`undefined`/`''` explicitly first.

Related: **use-before-declare is not a parse error.** `KEY` was declared several
hundred lines below a use in an outer scope, and every render failed for hours.
esbuild parses it happily. Anything the params snapshot needs must be resolved
at the top of the handler.
