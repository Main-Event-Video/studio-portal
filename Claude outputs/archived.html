Three things to check

1. Archived clients keep everything — opening one still gives you the full workspace, Montage Maker and all. They are only moved, not limited.

2. The row stays put at the bottom whether it is open or shut, so the live list never jumps around under your cursor.

3. If nothing is archived the row is not there at all, rather than an empty "Archived · 0".

4. Click Client, Event or Last upload to sort; click again to flip it. Sorting happens inside each group, so archived clients stay archived at the bottom.

5. Aaron Vance has no event date and has never uploaded — he stays at the BOTTOM whichever way the arrow points, so a date sort never opens with a wall of blanks.

6. New Client starts CLOSED. It is the first thing on the page and you use it rarely, so it was pushing the client list down every time you opened the admin. Click the pill to open it; it closes itself again after a client is created.
