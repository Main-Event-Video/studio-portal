Your assumption is half right — worth reading

The video FILE is never rendered into the montage. But its SLOT already is: each video becomes a full-frame chroma-green gap at exactly that position in the export, for you to key the real clip into. So the videos are not omitted from the montage — only from this screen, which is why you cannot currently see where the gaps land.

One thing you may not know about that gap

It lasts ONE PHOTO BEAT — the same duration as a photo — not the length of the video. A 40-second speech gets the same 3-second green hole as a 4-second clip. You would be stretching or trimming to fit in your edit. Say the word if the gap should instead run the video's real length.

Three rules in the mockup

1. Videos carry NO photo number. The numbering is what drives the montage's selection, so a video must not shift it — the photos still read 1, 2, 3, 4 straight past the video.

2. They are not editable and not draggable here. Their order comes from the client's timeline; this screen shows it, it does not set it.

3. The dashed green outline is deliberately the chroma green the gap actually renders as, so the marker and the thing it stands for look like each other.
